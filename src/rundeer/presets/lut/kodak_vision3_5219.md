# Kodak Vision3 5219 500T — LUT Reference

## Overview

Kodak Vision3 5219 is a 35mm tungsten-balanced (3200K) negative motion picture film rated at EI 500. It is the defining stock of contemporary Hollywood cinematography, used on everything from intimate dramas to large-scale action features. Its combination of wide exposure latitude, fine grain for a 500-speed stock, and beautiful shadow-to-highlight gradation makes it the reference point for "cinematic" imagery.

---

## Tone Curve Characteristics

### Shadows (0–20% luminance)
- **Foot shape:** Long, gradual toe — shadows open up softly rather than crushing hard.
- **Minimum density (D-min):** Lifted to approximately +12–15 IRE above true black; blacks land in a warm dark chocolate-brown rather than pure black.
- **Shadow color:** Warm reddish-brown base from the orange mask of the negative. On positive print, this manifests as a very slight warmth in deep shadows.
- **Detail retention:** Exceptional shadow latitude; two stops underexposed detail remains recoverable.

### Midtones (20–70% luminance)
- **Gamma:** Approximately 0.60 — a gentle, film-like roll rather than a linear ramp.
- **Skin tones:** Benchmark-accurate. Caucasian skin renders at roughly 55–65 IRE, with a warm amber quality. Deep skin tones hold separate from shadows clearly.
- **Color saturation:** Moderate and organic. No channel is aggressively boosted; the look is "real" rather than punchy.
- **Green channel:** Slightly compressed relative to red and blue — prevents foliage from going electric.

### Highlights (70–100% luminance)
- **Shoulder shape:** Very gradual, long shoulder compression. Highlights roll off naturally rather than clipping hard; specular lights clip last.
- **White point:** Creamy rather than pure white; approximately a -5 tint of warm cream.
- **Highlight color:** Neutral to very slightly warm. No significant hue shift in the shoulder — one of the stock's prized qualities.

---

## Per-Channel Adjustments

| Channel | Shadow Offset | Midtone Gamma | Highlight Gain |
|---------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| Red     | +8 IRE        | +0.04         | 0.0            |
| Green   | +5 IRE        | 0.0           | -0.02          |
| Blue    | +3 IRE        | -0.03         | -0.04          |

The net result in shadows: warm brown. In midtones: naturalistic with slightly warm bias. In highlights: neutral.

---

## Saturation

- **Global saturation:** ~1.05× — only mildly boosted. The stock is not punchy.
- **Skin hue range (orange-red):** +0.08 saturation — skin tones hold richness.
- **Cyan-blue range:** -0.06 — skies remain believable and not over-rendered.
- **Green range:** -0.05 — prevents foliage over-saturation.

---

## Grain Characteristics

- **Type:** Silver halide clumping, luminance-weighted (heaviest in shadows and upper-midtones, finer in highlights).
- **Grain size:** Fine for a 500-speed stock. At 2K scan, grain is barely perceptible in properly exposed areas.
- **Color grain:** Minimal chroma grain variance — one of the stock's modern improvements over Vision2.
- **Suggested digital equivalent:** Luminance noise σ=0.012 in shadows, σ=0.006 in highlights. No HF chroma noise.

---

## Use Cases

- Dramatic features, prestige TV, any scene where "cinematic but natural" is the goal.
- Night exteriors, dimly lit interiors (excellent shadow latitude).
- Skin-heavy close-ups — the stock's greatest strength.
- Action sequences — the grain stays fine even in fast motion.

---

## Pairing Notes

- **Complementary grade:** A mild warm lift in the grade (CCT ~200K warmer) plays to the stock's tungsten origins beautifully.
- **Avoid:** Heavy teal-and-orange over this stock — it fights the organic warmth. Let the stock breathe.
- **Works well with:** Anamorphic lens flare (the orange mask flatters streak colors). Subtle vignette.
- **Reference films:** *Blade Runner 2049*, *The Revenant*, *No Country for Old Men* (various Kodak stocks across those, but 5219 aesthetic).
