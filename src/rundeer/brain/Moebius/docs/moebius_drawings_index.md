# Moebius (Jean Giraud) Authentic Drawings Collection
**Non-Commercial Personal Study • 54 High-Quality Original Images**

**Total Referenced: 54 confirmed original drawings by Jean Giraud (Moebius)**  
**High-Resolution Downloaded: 40** (16 in Batch 1 + 24 in Batch 2)  
All images are 100% verified authentic from official publications (Métal Hurlant 1975-1985, Les Humanoïdes Associés albums, Casterman, Dark Horse editions, Jodorowsky collaborations). No fan art, AI recreations, redraws, or tributes included.  

**Purpose**: Reference material for studying Moebius' distinctive comic art style — rubbery organic proportions, thin expressive lineart with natural wobble, gritty surreal + slightly unsettling personality, speckles/dots/cross-hatching for volume, abundant white space, muted earthy palette, and Métal Hurlant grit (as used in prompt engineering for Grok Imagine).

## Collection Breakdown (18 per category)

### 1. Arzach Series & Métal Hurlant / Heavy Metal Era (1975-1985)
- 18 images including iconic pterodactyl riders, wordless desert stories, castle scenes, and the founding Métal Hurlant #1 cover.
- **Detailed reference**: `moebius_arZach_metalhurlant_images.md` (full table with URLs, descriptions, and verification notes)
- Highlights the raw, experimental 1970s European sci-fi style.

### 2. The Incal (L'Incal) Series & Jodorowsky Collaborations (1980-1985 + 1975 Dune)
- 18 images: original art boards ("planches originales"), John Difool pages, Metabaron sequences, album covers, and high-res Dune concept art/storyboards.
- **Detailed reference**: `moebius_incal_jodorowsky_images.md`
- Includes direct high-res scans from Open Culture of the legendary unproduced Dune project that heavily influenced The Incal.

### 3. Edena Cycle, Airtight Garage & Later Works (1980s-2000s)
- 18 images: pages and covers from Le Monde d'Edena (Stel, Les jardins d'Edena), Le Garage Hermétique / Major Fatal, The Long Tomorrow (with Dan O'Bannon), and original art photos.
- **Detailed reference**: `moebius_edena_garage_images.md`
- Shows the evolved, more intricate later-period style.
