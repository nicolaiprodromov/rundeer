## 3. Additional Notes on Technique, Proportions, Space, and Rendering (Synthesized from Sources + Cross-References)
- **Linework/Texture/Volume (Hatching/Stippling context):** While many sources emphasize "clear, strong lines without hatching" for the foundational Gir/ligne claire style, Moebius evolved toward more complex rendering. Stout highlights "astonishingly complex" pen work and "fine pen work." Sources note intricate details and ability to render objects effectively (implying hatching, parallel lines, or stippling/dots for texture and volume in sci-fi machinery, organic surfaces, or shading). Reddit discussions (r/moebius_giraud_study) observe Moebius' adeptness at hatching for volume while maintaining a casual, sketch-like quickness in lines.  
- **Proportions & Character Personality:** Organic forms, elongated/elegant or bizarre figures in surreal works (e.g., Arzach riders, Incal characters with mystical/unsettling gazes or mutated features). "Strange sublimity," "dreamlike," "mysterious and intrigue," "psychosexual realms" evoke unsettling or otherworldly personalities. Elongated proportions common in tall, slender sci-fi figures or grotesque exaggerations in fantasy.  
- **Negative/White Space & Flat 2D Comic Feel:** Expansive landscapes and "otherworldly" compositions rely on masterful negative space for scale, isolation, and surreal mood (e.g., vast deserts, empty skies in Arzach or Long Tomorrow). Inherent to bandes dessinées: bold outlines, graphic flatness with implied depth via line and minimal shading, strong 2D panel composition. "Clear strong (out)lines" and "flat" graphic tradition support "flat 2D comic feel."  
- **Overall Descriptors for Prompts:** "Intricate yet economical linework," "fluid/organic flowing lines," "precise outlines with perfect line weight," "surreal organic forms and expansive landscapes," "dreamlike atmosphere with mystery and intrigue," "coherent yet imaginative functional worlds," "strange sublimity and unsettling character presence."
