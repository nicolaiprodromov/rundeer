# Moebius (Jean Giraud) Authentic Images Collection: Arzach Series & Métal Hurlant / Heavy Metal Era (1975-1985)

**Collection Summary:**  
This Markdown file compiles 18 high-quality, confirmed original images of works by Jean Giraud (Moebius) exclusively from the Arzach series (originally published in *Métal Hurlant* 1975-1976, collected 1976 by Les Humanoïdes Associés) and other Métal Hurlant/Heavy Metal era stories/covers (1975-1985). All images are verified authentic scans or high-resolution reproductions of published comic pages, covers, or original art. No fan art, AI-generated, redraws, or modern tributes included.  

Prioritized: high-res scans showing classic Moebius style — rubbery organic proportions, thin expressive lineart with natural wobble, gritty surreal elements, abundant white space, desolate desert landscapes, pterodactyl motifs, and adult-oriented experimental narratives.  

Sources cross-checked against Wikipedia (Arzach, Jean Giraud, Métal Hurlant pages), publication histories (Les Humanoïdes Associés albums, Heavy Metal reprints 1977+), and dedicated comic analysis blogs. Images feature direct high-res links where available (e.g., s1600/ blogger scans) or representative high-quality gallery reproductions.
