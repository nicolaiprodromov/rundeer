# 2D hand-drawn style of Jean Giraud Moebius

Exact artistic style of Jean Giraud Moebius (Métal Hurlant era, 1975-1985), featuring his signature fluid organic proportions with stylized elongated anatomy that feels alive and slightly strange, fine precise pen-and-ink linework that is spare and airy yet pristinely detailed with natural hand-drawn wobble and perfect line weight, expressive characterful face conveying personality through whimsy and quiet existential intensity rather than exaggeration, delicate stippling speckles and economical cross-hatching creating volume and texture without heavy shadows, muted desaturated palette of pale greens faded blues cool mauves and raw earthy ochres with subtle pastel accents, gritty yet philosophical surreal atmosphere from Arzach and The Incal, flat 2D comic illustration with casually irrational dreamlike quality, everything drawn with functional coherence as if it could be built, both familiar and completely alien, no text no logos, professional high-quality European bande dessinée

- **Linework duality**: "Breathtakingly simple or astonishingly complex" — perfect line weight for the subject. Pen & ink mastery. Fine-lined yet bold, "pristinely detailed". "Spare and stately, nuanced, and airy". Clean ligne claire foundation evolved into intricate, fluid, organic rendering. Every element drawn has function — "if you built it, it would work".
- **Proportions**: Stylized, fluid, organic, "almost abstract". Elongated or bizarre figures with psychedelic quality. Not just grotesque — also elegant. "Oddly intersecting" forms. Rubbery, non-realistic anatomy that feels alive and intentional.
- **Volume & texture**: Delicate cross-hatching, stippling (speckles/dots), and economical parallel lines for texture and volume. Never heavy realistic shading. Hatching maintains a casual, sketch-like quickness. "Skillfully deployed areas of light and dark".
- **Character personality**: "The dark, the erotic, the whimsical, the intellectual, the bawdy, the scathing, the humorous, and the philosophical in countless combinations". "Whimsy and existential humility" like The Little Prince. Scruffy antiheroes, "fringe lowlifes." "Both familiar and completely alien". "Casually irrational, decidedly European" dream logic.
- **Atmosphere**: "Persistently strange sublimity". "Tangible future" with "sense of overload". "Quiet power" that disturbs and consoles simultaneously. Gritty Métal Hurlant adult edge (psychosexual, taboo-breaking via Crumb influence) balanced with philosophical depth and wonder.
- **Color**: Contrasting hues with pastel accents enhancing dreamlike atmosphere. Muted earthy tones for grit. Can range from desaturated to vivid — always serving surreal mood over realism.
- **World-building**: Cinematic framing — Vertical cities cramped with layered housing, floating traffic, seedy districts. Expansive desert horizons. Dense yet airy. Organic-futuristic blends where technology feels grown rather than manufactured. Dystopian.

## Key Style Rules (always apply)

**Linework:**
- Fine precise pen-and-ink lines — spare and airy, never heavy or overworked
- Natural hand-drawn wobble with perfect line weight for the subject
- Clean closed outlines with fluid organic quality (ligne claire roots)
- Can shift from breathtakingly simple to astonishingly complex within one piece
- Every line has function and purpose — nothing decorative or wasteful

**Proportions & Form:**
- Fluid, stylized, organic anatomy — elongated, rubbery, slightly strange but never random
- "Almost abstract" quality — proportions serve surreal mood over anatomical correctness
- Figures feel alive, expressive, and intentional — quirky but coherent
- Avoid both conventional beauty AND pure grotesquerie — aim for that strange middle ground

**Volume & Texture:**
- Stippling (dots/speckles) and delicate cross-hatching only — never solid fills or realistic shading
- Hatching maintains casual sketch-like quickness even when intricate
- Light and dark deployed skillfully but economically
- Texture suggests surfaces rather than rendering them fully

**Space & Composition:**
- Flat 2D comic feel with implied depth through line and minimal shading only

**Color Palette:**
- Subtle pastel accents for dreamlike atmosphere
- Never saturated, glossy, or digitally clean — always hand-colored feel
- Colors serve mood and surreal quality over realism

**Character & Atmosphere:**
- Personality through quiet intensity, whimsy, and existential depth — not shock or exaggeration
- "Both familiar and completely alien" — casually irrational European dream logic
- Gritty Métal Hurlant adult edge balanced with philosophical wonder
- Scruffy, lived-in, slightly seedy — antiheroes not heroes
- "Persistently strange sublimity" — disturbs and consoles simultaneously

**Hard Avoids:**
- Beautiful, polished, glossy, or digitally perfect aesthetics
- Heavy realistic shading, gradients, or 3D rendering
- Anime, manga, American superhero, or modern cartoon styles
- Cosmic/girly/cute/overly dreamy — aim for airy grit not prettiness
- Crowded compositions without breathing room
- Stiff, mechanical, or lifeless poses
- NO BORDERS OR FRAMES OR PASSEPARTOUT
- NO SIGNATURES OR NAMES OF THE ARTWORK
